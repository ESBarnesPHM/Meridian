Osteomyelitis Antibiotic Decision Aid

1. Is the patient clinically unstable?
If yes, prioritize prompt broad empiric therapy and sepsis management while cultures and source-control evaluation proceed.
↓
2. What does microbiology show?
Use Gram stain, rapid organism identification, and resistance markers. A detected organism with a relevant resistance gene not detected can support narrowing when the overall clinical picture is concordant.
↓
3. What does the local antibiogram show?
Choose an agent expected to cover the likely organism locally. Avoid broader therapy when reliable microbiology supports a narrower, effective option.
↓
4. Is the dose correct?
Verify the current measured weight, indication-specific dose, interval, renal function, allergy history, and maximum dose.
↓
5. What consultation and monitoring are needed?
Call Infectious Diseases after forming an evidence-based plan. Continue Orthopedic involvement and monitor clinical response, CRP, cultures, pain, perfusion, intake, and urine output.
